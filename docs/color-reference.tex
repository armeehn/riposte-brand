\ripsection{\ripmdhead{The rule this table exists to enforce}}

The brand's fixed pairings — \textbf{bone on pink}, \textbf{bone on teal} — are \riphi{brand-correct but not text-safe}. They land at 3.16:1 and 2.27:1. Keep them for chips, spec headers, section bars and large uppercase display, where they are the brand. The moment an accent fill has to carry a \textbf{sentence}, switch to the \ripmono{-deep} variant: same hue, same saturation, lightness dropped until bone clears 4.5:1.

\ripmono{ink} on \ripmono{orange} (8.12:1) is the one accent fill that is safe for prose as-is. That is why marigold takes ink text and the other two take bone.
