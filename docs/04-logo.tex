\ripsection{\ripmdhead{1 · The wordmark}}

\ripmono{RIPOSTE} set as a blackletter-derived wordmark with \textbf{esh} — the checkered jester figure, she/her — integrated into the letterforms, caught mid-riposte.

\textbf{She is part of the wordmark, not an ornament attached to it. Never crop her out, never separate her, never redraw her.}

\ripmdpath{\ripmono{assets/\allowbreak{}logo/\allowbreak{}riposte-\allowbreak{}bone.\allowbreak{}svg}} & \ripmono{\#f6f1e7} & ink fields, pink fields \\
\ripmdpath{\ripmono{assets/\allowbreak{}logo/\allowbreak{}riposte-\allowbreak{}pink.\allowbreak{}svg}} & \ripmono{\#f0477d} & accent moments only — rare \\
\end{ripmdtable}

Geometry: \ripmono{viewBox=\allowbreak{}"0 0 720 159"}, aspect ratio \textbf{4.528 : 1}.

Two harmonic variants exist for hero and sticker use, where esh's checker resolves into a wave field: \ripmdpath{\ripmono{riposte-\allowbreak{}harmonic-\allowbreak{}ink.\allowbreak{}svg}} and \ripmdpath{\ripmono{riposte-\allowbreak{}harmonic-\allowbreak{}bone.\allowbreak{}svg}}. And \ripmdpath{\ripmono{esh-ink.svg}} is esh standalone — for stickers, dies and physical goods only, \textbf{not} as a logo substitute.

On ink fields the production site applies \ripmono{filter:\allowbreak{} invert(1)} to the ink mark. That is equivalent to \ripmono{riposte-\allowbreak{}bone.\allowbreak{}svg} and is fine; prefer the real file where you can.


\ripsection{\ripmdhead{2 · Clear space}}

\textbf{One cap-height of the wordmark on all four sides.} Measure from the outermost ink, not from the SVG bounding box — the file carries no padding.

Nothing enters that zone: no text, no rule, no image edge, no checker band. The one sanctioned exception is the \textbf{topbar lockup}, where the letterspaced sub-line sits inside the lower clear space by design (see §4).


\ripsection{\ripmdhead{3 · Minimum size}}

\begin{ripmdtable}{X[0.81,l]X[1.19,l]}
\ripmdth{MEDIUM} & \ripmdth{MINIMUM WIDTH} \\
Screen & \textbf{110px} \\
Print & \textbf{28mm} \\
Embroidery / moulded / engraved & \textbf{40mm} — below this, use \ripmono{esh-ink.svg} alone \\
\end{ripmdtable}

Below the minimum, esh's checker fills merge and the mark turns to mud. There is \textbf{no simplified small-size variant} and \textbf{no monogram}. If a favicon or app icon is needed, that's an open task — do not improvise one by cropping the wordmark.


\ripsection{\ripmdhead{4 · The lockup}}

\ripcodeopen
\ripcodesize{9.0}{13.95}
\begin{ripcodeblock}
        RIPOSTE
L A B O R A T O R I E S   I N C .
\end{ripcodeblock}
\ripcodesizereset\ripcodeclose

\begin{ripbullets}
\item Sub-line: uppercase, weight \textbf{400} (not bold — the mark carries the weight)
\item Tracking \textbf{\ripmono{.55em}} (\ripmono{-\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}tracking-\allowbreak{}wordmark})
\item Cap height ≈ \textbf{1/8} of the wordmark's cap height
\item \textbf{Optically centred.} \ripmono{.55em} tracking adds trailing space after the final character, so subtract about half the tracking from the right or it sits visibly left of centre.
\end{ripbullets}

\textbf{Topbar variant} (production site): mark at \ripmono{110px}, then a stacked cell — \ripmono{Laboratories Inc.\allowbreak{}} over \ripmono{RL-\allowbreak{}000 · Rev.\allowbreak{}A} — separated by a dashed interior rule. This is the only lockup that pairs the mark with document-control chrome.

\ripsection{\ripmdhead{6 · Misuse}}

Do not:

\begin{ripbullets}
\item \textbf{Recolour} it outside the three fills. No gradients. No photo fills. No outline-only.
\item \textbf{Distort} it. Scale proportionally; the aspect ratio is 4.528:1 and stays there.
\item \textbf{Rotate} it. The only rotation in the system is \ripmono{.stamp} at \ripmono{-2deg}, and that's a stamp.
\item \textbf{Crop esh out}, isolate her \riphi{as the logo}, or redraw her.
\item \textbf{Add effects} — glow, bevel, blur, drop shadow other than \ripmono{--mark-drop}.
\item \textbf{Re-typeset} it. The wordmark is artwork, not JetBrains Mono. Never set "RIPOSTE" in the brand font and call it the logo.
\item \textbf{Put it inside a shape.} No circles, no rounded containers, no badges. Radius is 0 and the mark needs no holder.
\item \textbf{Pair it with another wordmark} without a \ripmono{2px} ink rule and one clear-space unit between them.
\end{ripbullets}


\ripsection{\ripmdhead{7 · Company name in text}}

\begin{ripbullets}
\item Full legal: \textbf{Riposte Laboratories Inc.}
\item Second reference: \textbf{Riposte Labs}, or \textbf{Riposte}
\item Never: "RipostéLabs", "riposte labs" mid-sentence, "RL" as a standalone brand

\item \textbf{esh} is lowercase, always, even sentence-initial. She/her.
\item Domain: \ripmono{ripostelabs.\allowbreak{}xyz}
\end{ripbullets}
