\ripsection{\ripmdhead{1 · Font}}

Install \textbf{JetBrains Mono} (400 / 700 / 800) on every machine that will open the file, or \textbf{embed it} — Word and PowerPoint both support font embedding, and the SIL OFL permits it.

\riphi{File → Options → Save → Embed fonts in the file} (Windows). On Mac, embedding is unavailable in some versions — export to PDF for distribution.

\textbf{Fallback if you can't install:} \textbf{Consolas}, which ships with Office everywhere. Note the substitution.

\ripsection{\ripmdhead{3 · PowerPoint}}

Widescreen 16:9. Background \ripmono{\#F6F1E7}, text \ripmono{\#1D1A17}.

\begin{ripmdtable}{X[1.73,l]X[0.79,l]X[0.99,l]X[0.94,l]X[0.55,l]}
\ripmdth{ROLE} & \ripmdth{SIZE} & \ripmdth{WEIGHT} & \ripmdth{CASE} & \ripmdth{SPACING} \\
Cover statement & 90–110 & ExtraBold & UPPER & −1 \\
Slide title & 44–54 & Bold & UPPER & 1.5 \\
Sub-heading & 28 & Bold & UPPER & 2 \\
Body & 22–24 & Regular & Sentence & 0 \\
Label / chrome & 12–14 & Bold & UPPER & 2.5 \\
\end{ripmdtable}

PowerPoint's character spacing is in \textbf{points}, not em. Multiply the em value by the font size: at 12pt, \ripmono{.1em} → \textbf{1.2pt}; at 44pt, \ripmono{.03em} → \textbf{1.3pt}. \riphi{Font → Advanced → Spacing: Expanded By.}


\ripsubsection{\ripmdhead{Slide masters to build}}

\begin{ripnumbers}
\item \textbf{Cover} — mark, display statement, corner marks (\ripmono{DOC NO.\allowbreak{} RL-\allowbreak{}000-\allowbreak{}A}, \ripmono{REV. A})
\item \textbf{Section} — ink fill, \ripmono{SEC.NN} chip, uppercase title
\item \textbf{Content} — chip + title + slash tag, 2.25pt rule beneath, content area
\item \textbf{Quote} — 6pt pink left bar, bold 32pt
\item \textbf{Closing} — colophon: ink rectangle left, tri-band right
\end{ripnumbers}


\ripsubsection{\ripmdhead{Shape defaults}}

\begin{ripbullets}
\item \textbf{Rounded corners → 0.} Use the rectangle, never the rounded rectangle. If you must use a rounded one, drag the yellow handle fully to the left.
\item \textbf{Shadows off.} \riphi{Format Shape → Effects → Shadow → No shadow}, on every element. Then right-click a correctly-formatted shape → \textbf{Set as Default Shape}.
\item \textbf{Hard offset instead:} duplicate the shape, fill with an accent, send backward, nudge right 4px and down 4px.
\item \textbf{Lines:} 2.25pt structural, 0.75pt dashed dividers.
\end{ripbullets}


\ripsubsection{\ripmdhead{Tri-band}}

Rectangle → \riphi{Format Shape → Fill → Gradient}, angle \textbf{105°}, six stops: pink @0\%, pink @36\%, marigold @36\%, marigold @64\%, teal @64\%, teal @100\%. The doubled stops are what make the boundaries hard.

Line spacing 1.5, space after 8pt, margins 20mm. Page colour \ripmono{\#F6F1E7} — but note Word won't print a background colour unless \riphi{Options → Display → Print background colours and images} is on. For anything going to press, put the bone on the stock instead (\ripdocref{\ripmono{.\allowbreak{}.\allowbreak{}/\allowbreak{}docs/\allowbreak{}08-\allowbreak{}print.\allowbreak{}md}}{RL-BRAND-001-PRINT}).

\item \textbf{Footer:} \ripmono{© 2026 RIPOSTE LABORATORIES INC.\allowbreak{}} left, page number right, 8pt

\end{ripbullets}


\ripsection{\ripmdhead{5 · Excel}}

\begin{ripbullets}
\item Header row: ink fill, bone bold uppercase text
\item No banded fills. A 0.5pt bottom border per row instead
\item Conditional formatting: pass \ripmono{\#12B795}, warn \ripmono{\#FE9A0D}, fail \ripmono{\#F0477D}, always paired with a \ripmono{✓ ! ✕} glyph in an adjacent column
\item Charts: theme accents 1–3 give the rotation automatically. Turn off gridlines, gradients, 3-D and shadows. Set every series border to 2.25pt ink
\end{ripbullets}
