The rule that gets broken Bone on bright pink is 3.16:1; bone on bright teal is 2.27:1. Both are brand-correct and both fail AA for body text — they are legal on chips, spec headers, section bars and large uppercase display, and nowhere else. On bone pages, marigold (1.90:1) and teal (2.27:1) cannot be text at all. Full matrix in docs/pdf/docs-color-reference.pdf.

SEC.05

Ink field

// data-theme="dark"

Ink field: bone text, bone 2px rules, dashed dividers at #5c554c, and the working accent flips pink → teal.

Every pattern reads semantic variables, so it inverts for free. If a component doesn't invert, it hardcoded a colour where it should have used --text or --accent.
